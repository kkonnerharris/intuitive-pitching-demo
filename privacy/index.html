Privacy policy

This explains what the Intuitive Pitching app keeps about athletes, why, who can see it, and how to get it deleted. The short version: we keep only what coaching needs, we never sell it or show ads, and you can ask for all of it to be deleted.

Who we are

Intuitive Pitching is a softball pitching development program that trains at Rise Performance 618. The coach runs this app for the athletes in the program. For any privacy question or request, tell your coach (a contact email is to be added).

What we collect

WhatWhy
Profile: name, graduation year or age group, throwing hand, position, school or teamTo keep each athlete's record and grade her against her own age group
PitchLogic ball data and 4D Motion body data, including the original export files the coach uploadsTo show sessions, trends and report cards. We keep the original file so the numbers can always be checked against it.
Coach notes, goals, report cards, grade cards and training plansCoaching. Private coach notes are never shown to the athlete.
Movement screens, and (only with consent) health, blood work and neurological assessmentsTo plan training safely around injuries and health
Consent records: the parent or guardian's name and the dates consent forms were signedTo show consent is on file. The app keeps the dates, not the signed forms.
An activity log of who viewed or changed a record, and whenSecurity, and to show who saw health information

We don't collect payment details, contact lists, precise location or anything from your phone. The app has no ads, analytics or trackers.

Athletes under 18

Many of our athletes are minors, some under 13. For an athlete under 18, or whose age isn't recorded yet, the coach records a parent or guardian's consent, and the app won't accept her health, blood work or neurological results until that consent and health-data consent are recorded. A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete their child's information, or withdraw consent, at any time. To do that, tell your coach (a contact email is to be added).

Health information

Health, blood work and neurological results are visible only to the coaching and clinical staff, unless the coach chooses to share a specific result with the athlete. They're used only to shape training. They aren't diagnoses, and nothing in the app is medical advice.

Who we share it with

  • Nobody for money. We never sell or rent athlete information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Hosting providers that store and run the app for us, under their own security and privacy terms. They don't use the data for their own purposes.
  • The law: only if we're legally required to.

PitchLogic and 4D Motion data comes from files the coach exports and uploads. The app doesn't connect to those companies' accounts or send anything back to them.

How long we keep it

  • An athlete's record is kept while she trains with us, and deleted when she or her parent asks.
  • Uploads the coach previews but never confirms are removed within a day.
  • A team export file shared by several athletes is kept only while another athlete's sessions still come from it.
  • The activity log keeps that an event happened. When a player is deleted, her name and the details of her log entries are removed.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • See or correct it: athletes see their own report card, goals, plans and shared results in the app. For anything else, tell your coach (a contact email is to be added).
  • Delete it: in the athlete view, use Request data deletion under "Your data", or tell your coach (a contact email is to be added). The coach then deletes the athlete's profile, sessions, goals, assessments, reports and uploaded files.
  • Withdraw consent: tell the coach, and no new health data will be added.

Cookies and browser storage

The app doesn't use cookies. See the cookie policy for the two things it saves in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change, and the coach will tell athletes and parents before the change applies to data already collected.
