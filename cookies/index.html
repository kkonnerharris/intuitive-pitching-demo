Cookie policy

The app doesn't set cookies, and it has no analytics, advertising or tracking. Fonts and images are served from the app itself, so no other company learns you visited.

What it saves in your browser

Two things are saved in your browser's local storage, only so the app works the way you left it. They stay on your device and aren't sent anywhere.

NameWhat it holdsHow long
ip-demo-role-v2Which view you picked (coach, athlete or clinician) and which athlete, so the app reopens thereUntil you clear it
ip-scorecard-draft-v1The grade card a coach is working on, so work isn't lost if the page closes. It can include an athlete's name and grades.Until the next grade card replaces it, or you clear it

If the app is password-protected, your browser may remember the password. That's your browser's own feature, not a cookie from us.

Why there's no cookie banner

A consent banner is for cookies and storage used for things like analytics or advertising. This app only saves what you need for a feature you're using, so there's nothing to accept or refuse.

Clearing it

Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove both. On a shared computer, coaches should clear this site's data before walking away. Questions? tell your coach (a contact email is to be added).
