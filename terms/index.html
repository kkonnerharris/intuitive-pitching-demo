Terms of service

These are the terms for using the Intuitive Pitching app. By using it, you agree to them. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian agrees for you.

Who can use it

The app is for athletes in the Intuitive Pitching program, their parents or guardians, and the coaching and clinical staff. The coach adds each athlete. Athletes under 18 are added only with a parent or guardian's consent (see the privacy policy).

Fees and refunds

The app is free for athletes in the program. It doesn't take payments, has no subscriptions and no hidden fees, so there is nothing in the app to refund. Training fees are agreed directly with Intuitive Pitching, not through the app.

Coaching, not medical advice

  • Report cards, grades, findings and programs are coaching tools. They come from the coach's ranges and the app's written rules, and each finding shows the rule behind it. The app doesn't use AI to make them.
  • Numbers come from PitchLogic and 4D Motion as their files export them. Sensor readings can be off, so read a single session with care.
  • Nothing in the app is medical advice. Stop and see a doctor or athletic trainer if something hurts. Health results in the app are there to plan training around, not to diagnose anything.

Using it fairly

  • Only look at your own record, or the athletes you coach.
  • Don't upload files you don't have the right to share.
  • Don't try to get around access controls or overload the app.

Athletes' data belongs to them. We use it only as the privacy policy describes, and delete it when asked.

Accessibility

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. The app works with a keyboard alone, form fields have labels, the focused element is always outlined, text meets AA contrast, and motion is reduced if your device asks for it. The home page video is decorative, and the same information is written out on the page. If something is hard to use, tell your coach (a contact email is to be added) and we'll fix it or get you the information another way.
